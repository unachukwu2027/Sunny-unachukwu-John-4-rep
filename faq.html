About the Campaign

CONSISTENCY 2027 is the campaign identity associated with Barr. Sunny John Unachukwu, Esq., for the Federal House of Representatives seat representing Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency in Imo State.

The campaign slogan is "THE CHOICE IS NOW. THE CHOICE IS CLEAR." Another campaign message is "CONSISTENCY TODAY, BETTER TOMORROW!"

Visit the Manifesto page to read the campaign's stated priorities and proposals. Check official campaign communications for the latest confirmed information.

Projects & Development

Visit the Projects & Priorities page. Proposed priorities are not the same as completed or funded projects. Confirmed project details should be published only after verification.

Use the Contact page to submit a community concern or development suggestion. Include the community name and a clear description of the issue.

Volunteering & Participation

Visit the Volunteer page and complete the registration form. Follow any additional instructions provided by an authorized campaign representative.

The current demonstration form stores information in the browser's local storage on the device where it is completed. It does not automatically send submissions to the campaign team or share them with other devices. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

Copy the website address from your browser and share it with others. You can also use the share feature on pages where it is available. Always share accurate information from official campaign sources.

Events & Updates
