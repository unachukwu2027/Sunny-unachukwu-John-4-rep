Know Your Voting Rights

Understand the voting process, consult official election information, and help make elections peaceful, informed, and participatory.

Official INEC Resources Test Your Knowledge

Why Voter Education Matters

Voter education helps citizens understand election procedures, check official information, protect their voting rights, and participate peacefully in the democratic process.

This page provides general educational information. For current requirements, dates, eligibility decisions, and official procedures, always consult the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC).

Nonpartisan information: Every eligible voter should be able to access accurate election information and make an independent choice. Your vote is your personal decision.

Important Things Every Voter Should Know

🪪 Voter Registration

Check official INEC information about voter registration, eligibility, registration updates, and any applicable deadlines.

Visit INEC registration portal

📍 Find Your Polling Unit

Check your assigned polling unit through official INEC resources before election day. Do not rely only on forwarded messages.

Open INEC polling-unit locator

🗳️ On Election Day

Follow instructions from authorised election officials, observe the official process, and ask officials if you need procedural help.

🔒 Protect Your Choice

Make your voting decision independently. Do not allow threats, intimidation, or pressure to determine your choice.

🚫 Reject Vote Buying

Do not sell your vote or participate in electoral fraud. Consult official authorities for guidance on reporting suspected electoral offences.

🤝 Keep Elections Peaceful

Respect other voters, avoid spreading unverified election claims, and do not participate in violence or intimidation.

Before You Go to Vote

  1. Check your registration status using official INEC information.
  2. Confirm your assigned polling unit and its location.
  3. Review official guidance on accepted identification and voting procedures.
  4. Check official announcements about election dates and opening times.
  5. Follow the lawful instructions of election officials.
  6. Keep your voting choice private and independent.

Procedures and requirements can change. Confirm the latest instructions directly with INEC before election day.
