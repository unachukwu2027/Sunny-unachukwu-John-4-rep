A Message of Welcome

Welcome to CONSISTENCY 2027

A platform for information, engagement, dialogue, and a shared conversation about the future of Mbaitoli and Ikeduru.

Dear People of Mbaitoli and Ikeduru,

It is with respect and a strong sense of responsibility that we welcome you to the CONSISTENCY 2027 campaign website of Barr. Sunny John Unachukwu, Esq.

This website has been created to serve as an information centre where residents, community stakeholders, young people, women, professionals, traditional institutions, and other interested citizens can learn about the candidate, explore the campaign's proposed priorities, and follow campaign information.

Our message is built around consistency, responsible representation, public engagement, and a commitment to listening to the concerns and aspirations of the people.

We believe that meaningful representation begins with listening, continues through open dialogue, and must be guided by accountability and the public interest. The challenges and opportunities facing our communities deserve thoughtful discussion and practical, transparent approaches.

Through this platform, visitors can explore the candidate's biography, review campaign priorities, read published updates, view campaign photographs, and find information about participating in campaign activities.

We invite you to explore the website, share your views respectfully, and stay informed as the campaign develops. Your ideas and concerns are important to the conversation about the future of our constituency.

Together, let us continue the conversation about service, development, opportunity, and responsible representation.

Our Areas of Focus

Priorities for Community Development

Explore proposed areas for public discussion and policy development for Mbaitoli and Ikeduru Federal Constituency.

🎓

Education

Support discussion of educational opportunities, learning facilities, skills development, and improved access to education.

🏥

Healthcare

Promote dialogue on accessible healthcare, community health services, and improved health awareness.

🛣️

Infrastructure

Prioritise discussion of roads, drainage, transportation, electricity, water access, and community infrastructure needs.

💼

Youth Empowerment

Encourage conversations about employability, vocational training, entrepreneurship, and opportunities for young people.

🌾

Agriculture

Support consideration of farming, food security, agricultural enterprise, and opportunities across the agricultural value chain.

🤝

Community Representation

Encourage open communication, responsible representation, accountability, and engagement with constituents.
