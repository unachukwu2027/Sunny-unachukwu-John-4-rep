Our Campaign Priorities

A people-centred approach to representation, opportunity, community development, and responsible public service.

Roads and Infrastructure

Advocate for improved community roads, drainage, transportation, and attention to essential infrastructure needs.

Youth Empowerment

Promote skills acquisition, entrepreneurship, digital learning, and access to opportunities for young people.

Education

Support advocacy for better learning environments, student opportunities, and access to educational information.

Women and Families

Encourage women's economic participation, enterprise development, and initiatives that support families and communities.

Healthcare and Welfare

Advocate for accessible healthcare, community health awareness, and attention to vulnerable residents.

Local Economy

Promote opportunities for farmers, traders, artisans, small businesses, and local economic development.
