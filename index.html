The 7-Point Agenda

These proposed priorities should be reviewed and confirmed by the campaign before publication as the candidate's official agenda.

1

Education and Skills

Promote access to quality education, skills development and opportunities for young people.

2

Youth Empowerment

Support initiatives that improve employability, entrepreneurship and community participation.

3

Healthcare

Advocate improved access to affordable healthcare and better health facilities.

4

Roads and Infrastructure

Promote attention to roads, public infrastructure and essential community services.

5

Agriculture and Enterprise

Encourage agricultural development, small businesses and local economic opportunities.

6

Accountability and Representation

Encourage transparent communication, constituency consultation and responsible representation.

7

Community Development

Advocate inclusive development that considers communities across the constituency.
