Meet the Candidate

Barr. Sunny John Unachukwu

This campaign presents Sunny John Unachukwu as a candidate for the Federal House of Representatives for the Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency, with a message centred on consistency, public service and a better tomorrow.

Leadership begins with listening. The goal is to promote accountable representation, work constructively with communities and keep people’s needs at the centre of public priorities.

  • Accountable leadership and transparent representation
  • Sustainable development and stronger communities
  • People-first service and inclusive opportunity
  • Unity, peace and collaborative progress
