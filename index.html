Service to the People

CONSISTENCY 2027 — A campaign focused on listening, representation and constructive community engagement.

A Campaign Built Around Consistency

CONSISTENCY 2027 represents a campaign message centered on dependable service, community engagement, accountability and a forward-looking approach to representation.

The campaign invites residents to explore its proposed priorities, share their concerns and participate in discussions about opportunities and challenges across Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency.

  • Community engagement and listening
  • Transparent and responsible representation
  • Development priorities informed by local needs
  • Respectful dialogue and public participation

The Vision

Proposed Development Priorities

Explore areas the campaign may prioritize for discussion and advocacy. These are proposed priorities, not claims that projects have already been completed or funded.

🛣️

Infrastructure

Advocate for attention to road connectivity, transport challenges and essential community infrastructure.

🎓

Education

Promote discussion about educational opportunities, school needs, skills development and youth learning.

🏥

Healthcare

Encourage attention to accessible healthcare services, community health needs and improved health awareness.

🌾

Agriculture and Livelihoods

Support dialogue on agricultural opportunities, livelihoods, local enterprise and economic participation.

💼

Youth and Enterprise

Explore ideas around vocational skills, entrepreneurship, employment pathways and business development.

🤝

People-Centred Representation

Encourage consultation, accountability and constructive engagement with residents and community groups.
