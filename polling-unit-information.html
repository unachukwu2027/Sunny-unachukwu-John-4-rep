How to Find Your Polling Unit

  1. Open the official polling-unit locator. Use the “Find My Polling Unit” button above.
  2. Follow the portal's instructions. Select the relevant state, local government area and ward if requested.
  3. Confirm your assigned location. Check the displayed polling-unit details carefully.
  4. Plan ahead. Confirm the location and check official INEC announcements before election day.

Election-Day Preparation

✔ Verify Your Details

Check your registration and polling-unit information using official INEC resources before election day.

✔ Follow Official Guidance

Check INEC announcements for the confirmed election date, opening arrangements and required voter identification.

✔ Vote Independently

Your vote is your choice. Make your decision freely and keep your ballot choice private.

Important: This page provides general voter information. It is not an INEC service and does not register voters or determine polling-unit assignments. Always verify details through official INEC channels. Never share your voter identification number, passwords or sensitive personal information with unofficial websites.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my assigned polling unit?

Open the official INEC polling-unit locator and follow its instructions to find your assigned location.

Can I use this campaign page to register to vote?

No. This page provides links to official resources. Use INEC's official voter portal for registration services and check which services are currently available.

Where can I confirm election dates?

Check the official INEC website for the current election timetable and any subsequent announcements.

What if my polling-unit details appear incorrect?

Contact INEC through its official website or contact channels for guidance on checking or correcting your registration details.
