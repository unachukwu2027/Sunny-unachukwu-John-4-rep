Privacy Policy

This page explains how information may be handled when you visit the CONSISTENCY 2027 campaign website and use its forms.

Important: Review and update this policy before publishing the website for public use. The website owner should confirm the actual hosting, analytics, form and data-storage arrangements.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the intended handling of information on the CONSISTENCY 2027 campaign website associated with Barr. Sunny John Unachukwu, Esq. It should be read alongside the website's actual features and any notices displayed when information is submitted.

2. Information You May Provide

Depending on which features are enabled, you may choose to enter information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Contact details, such as a phone number or email address.
  • Volunteer interests or availability.
  • Messages or other information you choose to submit through a form.

Please do not submit passwords, bank details, identity documents, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary website forms.

3. How the Volunteer Form Works

Local storage notice

The current volunteer form is designed to save submissions in the browser's local storage on the device being used. This means the saved information is not automatically transmitted to a campaign administrator or made available on other devices.

Information stored locally may remain on that device until it is deleted by the user, cleared through browser settings, or removed by other device or browser actions. Clearing browser data or changing devices may cause saved information to become unavailable.

4. How Information May Be Used

If the campaign later enables a shared online form or another data collection service, information provided through that feature may be used for the purpose explained when the information is collected, such as responding to an enquiry or coordinating a volunteer request. Any such use should be explained clearly before the feature is launched.

This page does not claim that locally stored volunteer records are currently being sent to the campaign team.

5. Cookies, Analytics and Third-Party Services

The website may be hosted by a third-party provider, such as GitHub Pages. The site owner should review the hosting provider's current privacy information to understand what technical information may be processed when visitors access the website.

This policy does not confirm that analytics, advertising trackers, or additional third-party form services are installed. If any are added, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

6. Sharing of Information

Information saved only in local storage is generally kept within the relevant browser on that device. The campaign should not describe such information as being received centrally unless a shared submission system is actually implemented.

If a future website feature sends information to another service, the campaign should explain the purpose and relevant service before collecting that information.

7. Protecting Your Information

Keep your device secure, use a screen lock where appropriate, and avoid entering personal information on shared devices. Anyone with access to an unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view information stored there.

No website or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can help protect your privacy by:

  • Choosing carefully what information you submit.
  • Avoiding the entry of sensitive personal information.
  • Using your browser's storage and privacy settings when appropriate.
  • Removing locally saved information when it is no longer needed, if the form provides that option.

If a shared online registration system is introduced, the campaign should provide an appropriate way for people to ask about access, correction or deletion of their information.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to encourage children to submit personal information without appropriate guidance. If a child has entered information by mistake, the responsible adult should review the available options for removing it from the device or contacting the website owner.

10. External Links

This website may link to other websites or social media platforms. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices. Review their policies before providing them with personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may need to change when website features, hosting arrangements, or information-handling practices change. The published version should be reviewed and updated whenever those arrangements materially change.

12. Contact and Privacy Questions

If you have a question about this policy, visit the website's Contact page for the available contact options. Official contact details should be added here once they have been confirmed by the website owner.
