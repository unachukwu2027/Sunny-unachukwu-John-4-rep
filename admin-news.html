Administrator Access

This dashboard uses your existing Supabase project. Administrator sign-in is required before news can be published securely.

An administrator account must first be created in Supabase Authentication. Do not share your password or publish it in your website code.

Publish a News Article

Publishing is disabled until secure administrator access and database permissions are configured. This prevents unauthorized visitors from publishing news to your campaign website.

Publishing Instructions

  1. Sign in using your authorized administrator account.
  2. Enter the headline, summary, image URL and full story.
  3. Publish only after the administrator permissions are configured.
  4. Published articles will appear on the public news page when news.html retrieves them from Supabase.

Image URLs must point directly to an image file accessible by your website. A normal image-hosting page link may not display as an image.
