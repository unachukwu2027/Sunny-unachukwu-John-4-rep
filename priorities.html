OUR CAMPAIGN PRIORITIES

Our proposed development priorities focus on opportunities, essential services and community development for the people of Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency, Imo State.

1

Education

Advocate for improved access to quality education, better learning environments, educational resources and opportunities for students.

2

Healthcare

Promote improved access to primary healthcare, community health services and public health awareness.

3

Roads and Infrastructure

Advocate for improved road networks and essential infrastructure that connects communities and supports trade.

4

Youth Empowerment

Support opportunities for vocational training, skills development, entrepreneurship and employment.

5

Agriculture

Encourage agricultural development, support for local farmers and opportunities for agribusiness.

6

Women and Family Development

Advocate for inclusive opportunities that support women, families and vulnerable members of the community.

7

Electricity and Water

Promote advocacy for improved electricity access, clean water and other essential community services.

8

Markets and Small Businesses

Encourage a better environment for small businesses, local traders and community-based economic activity.

9

Community Representation

Encourage consultation with constituents, transparency, accountability and attention to local development needs.

Important note: These are proposed campaign priorities, not claims that projects have already been completed, funded or approved. Specific commitments should be confirmed by the campaign.
