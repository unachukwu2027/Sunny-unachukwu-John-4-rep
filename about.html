Barr. Sunny John Unachukwu, Esq.

Barrister Sunny John Unachukwu, also known as Barrister John Sunny-Unachukwu, is a Nigerian legal practitioner and public figure associated with community development and political participation in Imo State.

He is from Iho-Dimeze in Ikeduru Local Government Area of Imo State. His public profile includes legal practice, philanthropic interests, and participation in the political process.

Professional Background

His professional background includes legal training and engagement with public affairs. For a complete account of his education, professional qualifications, career history, and public service, please visit the Biography page.

Community and Social Development

He is associated with the Okwadike Foundation. His public profile also reflects an interest in philanthropy and community-oriented activities.
