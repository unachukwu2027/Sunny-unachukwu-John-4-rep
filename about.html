Consistency 2027 Campaign

Sunny John Unachukwu, Esq., is presented by this campaign as a candidate seeking to represent the people of Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency in the Federal House of Representatives.

The campaign's central message is consistency: a commitment to people-focused representation, responsible leadership, constructive engagement, and a future in which communities can aspire to better opportunities.

The campaign invites residents, community leaders, young people, women, professionals, and other stakeholders to participate in conversations about the constituency's needs and priorities.

Our Foundation

The Values Behind Consistency 2027

These campaign values express the principles the campaign seeks to promote in public service and representation.

✓

Consistency

Maintaining a steady focus on community concerns, public engagement, and commitments made to the people.

♡

Service to the People

Keeping the interests, concerns, and aspirations of constituents at the centre of public representation.

◎

Accountability

Encouraging open communication, responsible leadership, and clear explanations of decisions and commitments.

↗

Development

Supporting constructive conversations about infrastructure, economic opportunity, education, and community progress.

✦

Unity

Respecting the diversity of communities and encouraging cooperation toward shared constituency goals.

◇

Public Engagement

Listening to residents and encouraging meaningful participation in discussions about the constituency's future.

Our Commitment

A Better Tomorrow Starts With Service

The campaign's priorities should be shaped by the real needs of the people of Mbaitoli and Ikeduru.

Listening to the People

Representation begins with understanding what communities need. The campaign encourages respectful engagement with residents, local groups, and other stakeholders.

  • Community dialogue and public engagement
  • Attention to constituency concerns
  • Respect for different opinions
  • Clear communication with residents

Building for the Future

The campaign seeks to promote practical discussions about opportunities that can contribute to long-term community development.

  • Youth opportunity and skills development
  • Support for enterprise and local livelihoods
  • Education and community wellbeing
  • Infrastructure and constituency development
