The Campaign Vision

Service to the People

Consistency Today, Better Tomorrow!

This page outlines the broad themes associated with the Consistency 2027 campaign: people-first service, accountability, youth empowerment, infrastructure, unity and sustainable development.


Effective representation begins with listening to the people, understanding local needs and working with communities to advance practical priorities. The campaign invites constituents to participate in constructive conversations about the future of Mbaitoli/Ikeduru.

Our Priorities

Building a Better Tomorrow

Four broad areas for public discussion and community engagement.

♧

1. People-Centred Representation

Representation should listen to constituents and take community concerns seriously.

  • Encourage regular community engagement.
  • Promote accountability and openness.
  • Support inclusive participation in public discussions.
  • Communicate constituency concerns responsibly.
↗

2. Youth Empowerment

Young people need opportunities to learn, contribute and participate in community development.

  • Encourage vocational and digital skills initiatives.
  • Promote entrepreneurship and innovation.
  • Support access to information about opportunities.
  • Encourage youth participation in community affairs.
⌂

3. Infrastructure and Development

Sustainable development requires attention to essential infrastructure and local priorities.

  • Listen to communities about infrastructure needs.
  • Advocate for appropriate development priorities.
  • Encourage transparent use of public resources.
  • Support cooperation with relevant authorities.
🤝

4. Unity, Peace and Inclusion

Lasting progress is stronger when communities work together with mutual respect.

  • Promote peaceful and respectful dialogue.
  • Encourage cooperation across communities.
  • Respect diverse perspectives and experiences.
  • Support inclusive community participation.
◎

5. Community Development

Local needs should be understood through meaningful engagement and responsible planning.

  • Encourage community feedback and consultation.
  • Promote practical, needs-based discussions.
  • Support partnerships that benefit communities.
  • Track public priorities and communicate progress.
✓

6. Accountability and Service

Public service should be guided by responsibility, integrity and clear communication.

  • Encourage transparent communication.
  • Promote responsible public representation.
  • Listen to concerns and feedback.
  • Keep public priorities at the centre of engagement.
