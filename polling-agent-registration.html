Polling Unit Agent Registration

Interested in serving as a polling unit agent? Use this page to express your interest and provide your contact details for follow-up.

Please Read Before Registering

This is an expression-of-interest form for the campaign team. Completing it does not appoint you as a polling unit agent and does not provide INEC accreditation.

Do not submit your National Identification Number, Voter Identification Number, photographs of identity documents or other sensitive identification information through this form.

Only provide information you are comfortable sharing with the campaign contact responsible for this process.

Official INEC Polling-Agent Platform

INEC's 2027 portal states that authorized officials of recognized political parties submit and certify polling-agent nominations through the official electronic platform.

Contact your party secretariat or authorized party official for nomination and accreditation instructions.
